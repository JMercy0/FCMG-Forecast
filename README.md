# 🚀 Project Kickoff

## Project Title

**FMCG Global Demand Planning and Forecasting Using Machine Learning**

---

# Business Problem

## Background

Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) companies operate in highly dynamic markets where customer demand is influenced by pricing, promotions, seasonality, weather, regional preferences, and supply chain constraints. Poor demand forecasting often results in stockouts, overstocking, increased inventory costs, and lost sales opportunities.

This project leverages over **1.1 million daily sales records** collected across **15 countries**, **60+ cities**, and **350+ SKUs** to develop a data-driven demand forecasting framework that improves inventory planning and supply chain decision-making.

---

# Problem Statement

Current inventory planning relies heavily on historical sales trends and manual adjustments, making it difficult to respond to changing market conditions. The absence of accurate demand forecasts increases operational costs and reduces customer satisfaction.

This project seeks to identify the factors driving product demand and develop predictive models capable of forecasting future sales volumes while providing actionable business insights.

---

# Business Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Identify the major drivers of product demand.
2. Analyze sales performance across countries, cities, and distribution channels.
3. Quantify the impact of promotions and pricing on demand.
4. Forecast future demand for selected products.
5. Reduce stockouts and excess inventory.
6. Improve Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP).

---

# Research Questions

This project will answer the following questions:

1. Which products generate the highest sales?
2. Which countries contribute the highest revenue?
3. Which sales channels perform best?
4. How do promotions influence demand?
5. Does price significantly affect sales volume?
6. Which products experience the greatest demand volatility?
7. Can machine learning accurately forecast future demand?
8. Which variables have the greatest influence on demand?

---

# Success Metrics
